Why Existing Solutions Fell Short

DeHaat & Ninjacart digitized the chain.
They didn't restructure the economics.

Ninjacart
  • Optimized logistics, not farmer income
  • B2B only — farmer still gets a fraction
  • No financing — distress selling continues
  • Pure digital — no trust layer
DeHaat
  • Full-stack cost is unsustainable
  • Replaces middlemen with a tech middleman
  • Scale over depth — trust takes time
  • Limited cold-chain investment
Root Causes
  • Built tech before understanding farmer pain
  • VC growth model ≠ thin agri margins
  • Apps can't replace human trust
  • Ignored the credit-distress sales link
