The Growth Flywheel

Each element of the system strengthens every other element — creating a self-reinforcing growth cycle.

1
More farmers join
→ More raw materials become available
2
More raw materials
→ More products can be processed
3
More products available
→ Brand expands into more shops & markets
4
Brand becomes stronger
→ Consumer demand increases
5
More demand → More farmers want to join → Cycle repeats ↻
The farmer network, supply chain, processing network, brand, and consumer market continuously strengthen each other.
