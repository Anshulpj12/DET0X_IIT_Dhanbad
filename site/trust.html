priority_high Problem #1 — The Foundation

The Trust
Problem

Farmers have been exploited by middlemen for generations. They don't trust new organizations. Trust must be earned through proof, not promises.

Why Farmers Don't Trust

Every year, farmers face broken promises from traders, delayed payments, rigged scales, and exploitative pricing. Here's what they experience:

gavel

Broken Promises

Traders promise good prices at the start of the season, then offer far less when the crop is ready. Farmers have no recourse.

money_off

Delayed Payments

Even after selling, payments are delayed for weeks or months. Farmers are forced to borrow at 24-60% interest from moneylenders. Source: WDRA/RBI

groups_off

Unknown Third Parties

Produce is sent to unknown warehouses, handled by unknown transport. Farmers have zero visibility or control over their own crop.

lightbulb The DET0X Method

Trust Through Proof, Not Promises

Instead of asking thousands of farmers to trust us, we start small, prove results, and let successful farmers become our best advocates.

1

Select Small Group

Choose a small pilot group of farmers we can work with directly. No mass promises.

2

Deliver Real Results

Provide storage, transport, and selling support. Record everything: crop saved, wastage reduced, price received, profit gained.

3

Create Proof of Concept

Documented success becomes undeniable evidence. Real numbers from real farmers in their own community.

4

Trust-Based Expansion

Successful farmers recruit others from their community. Trust spreads through peer influence, not advertising.
