The Partner Warehouse Model

Instead of building every warehouse, DET0X identifies large farmers with unused storage capacity and converts them into trusted storage partners.

handshake

Profit-Sharing Model

Warehouse owners earn a percentage of profits from operations using their facilities — not a flat rental fee. Both parties benefit.

verified_user

Known & Trusted Network

Produce stays within a network of known farmers and agricultural partners — not unknown third-party warehouses. This strengthens trust.

shield

Quality Incentive

The warehouse partner has incentive to maintain proper storage because their reputation and financial benefit are tied to the system's success.
