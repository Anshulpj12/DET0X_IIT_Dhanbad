Judges' Walkthrough

How DET0X Works

A step-by-step walkthrough showing how we transform an unorganized agricultural system into an organized, farmer-centered ecosystem.

The Complete Value Chain

Follow the journey from a farmer's field to the consumer's table, organized under one system.

01
The Root Problem

Farmers Lack Reliable Storage

After harvesting, farmers face spoilage, pest damage, moisture issues, and quality loss because they have nowhere to store crops safely. This forces them to sell immediately — often at the lowest prices of the season, resulting in 30–40% income loss.

02
Start Small, Prove It

Select Pilot Farmers & Build Proof

We start with a small group of farmers, providing them complete storage, transport, and selling support. We carefully record every result — crop saved, wastage reduced, price received, profit generated. This becomes our proof of concept.

03
Trust-Based Growth

Expand Through Farmer References

Successful farmers become living proof. Instead of approaching thousands, we use real results to show other farmers the system works. Existing successful farmers influence new farmers from their own community to join — creating organic, trust-based expansion.

04
Infrastructure Without CapEx

Partner Warehouse Network

Instead of building warehouses, we partner with large farmers who already own unused storage capacity. They earn a profit-share from operations using their facilities — no rental fees, mutual benefit. Produce stays within trusted farmer networks, not unknown third-party warehouses.

05
Organized Logistics

Transportation & Employment

We organize collection from fields, transport to warehouses, inter-warehouse movement, and final delivery to buyers. Truck drivers currently working only 10–15 days/month get stable ~25 days/month employment with accommodation and support — converting an irregular workforce into organized logistics.

06
Smart Market Decisions

Market Analysis & Strategic Selling

We don't automatically sell everything immediately. We analyze where the crop can receive the best price, where demand is highest, whether it should be sold raw or processed. Every decision is driven by market demand, regional requirements, and profitability — not desperation.

07
Beyond Raw Commodities

Value-Added Processing

Wheat becomes flour. Mustard becomes oil. Chillies become spices. Grains become packaged products. We partner with existing manufacturers to convert raw crops into higher-value finished products — creating 3–5x value multiplication and additional employment.

08
One Unified Brand

Common Brand & Consumer Trust

All products carry the DET0X brand — representing authenticity, traceability, farmer participation, quality standards, and transparent sourcing. Consumers associate the brand with products that originate from genuine farmers, potentially commanding a premium price.

09
Reach Every Shelf

Retail Distribution & Growth Flywheel

Branded products flow into local shops and retailers. More farmers join → more raw materials available → more products processed → brand grows stronger → more shops stock it → consumer demand increases. This creates a self-reinforcing growth cycle that continuously strengthens every part of the ecosystem.
